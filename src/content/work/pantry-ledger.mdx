## The problem

Our kitchen had zero visibility. Groceries ran out silently, duplicates piled up and shopping trips relied on memory.

> The problem wasn't shopping. It was visibility.

I had no answer to three simple questions:

1. What do we actually have right now?
2. What do we use the most, and the least?
3. What's out of stock before I leave for the store?

## The spark

I went to the Design x AI Meetup in Chennai on a Saturday. The organisers showed real AI workflows: what they tried, what broke and what stuck. There was no hype, and it made building feel doable.

So I tried it on a very ordinary problem. By the next week I had shipped my first app.

## What I built

Pantry Ledger is a home grocery inventory and analytics app.

- **Inventory.** Every item is tracked with a photo, a category and a quantity.
- **Weekly audits.** A five-minute audit each week logs what we consumed.
- **Shopping list.** Out-of-stock items turn red and populate a shopping list automatically, grouped by category.
- **Dashboard.** Most-used items, rarely touched ones and monthly trends.

You don't need to be a developer to build something real. You need a genuine problem, patience and a willingness to debug your first `.env` file.

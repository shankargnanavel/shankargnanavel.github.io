## Overview

WELNS is an online booking marketplace that connects salons and spas with their customers. It tackles two frustrations people accept as normal: not knowing how long they will wait, and a booking process that takes too much effort.

## Discovery and research

The research phase had four parts:

- Competitive analysis of existing booking platforms
- A survey of how people book today
- Analysis of existing salon management systems
- Interviews with customers and with salon owners

## Five flows that make a booking

I structured the platform around the five steps of a booking, so each one leads naturally to the next:

1. Salon discovery
2. Service selection
3. Staff selection
4. Appointment scheduling
5. Payment

The design moved through low-fidelity wireframes, high-fidelity designs, then user testing and iteration.

## Key features

### Salon discovery

A location-based search helps people find salons nearby, with:

- Results based on geolocation
- Advanced filters
- Detailed salon profiles
- Services grouped by category

### Smart booking

I streamlined the booking flow to remove friction:

- Service selection with clear pricing
- Staff profiles and availability
- Real-time calendar integration
- A simple customer details form

### Wait-time management

This is the feature that sets WELNS apart:

- Real-time wait-time updates
- Queue position tracking
- Notifications
- Automated reminders

### Book now, pay later

Flexible payment makes booking easier to commit to:

- Secure payment processing
- Multiple payment methods
- Pay at the salon
- Gift vouchers

### Mobile-first build

The platform was designed for mobile users first:

- Touch-optimised interfaces
- Responsive layouts
- Performance optimisation
- Offline capabilities

## Testing and iteration

Testing covered four areas:

- Usability testing with target users
- A/B testing of key features
- Performance testing
- Accessibility testing
